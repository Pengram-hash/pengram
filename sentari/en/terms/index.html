Free plan

The free plan is offered with no time limit. It lets you work on up to 50 distinct cards a day per device, new words included, at most 15 of them new (up to 50 on your first day). It includes A1 to C1 vocabulary, three personal decks and voice review.

The free plan checks an online authorization once a day per device; reviews then continue offline until it expires. A new check does not give back the cards already worked on.

Premium

Premium removes the daily limits and the personal deck limit, and adds C2 vocabulary, theme selection, complete deck lists, all-English immersion and long-term projections, among other things. The included features are described in the app before any purchase. The monthly subscription, the annual subscription and the lifetime purchase unlock the same Premium.
