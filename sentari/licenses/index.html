Sources and licenses

Updated October 11, 2026. These notices are the ones shown in the app, under Settings, Data sources & licenses. They are kept in English because they are legal and bibliographic texts.

Sentari combines original educational content, written and edited for Sentari by Pengram, with openly licensed reference data. Every license below allows a commercial app; each one is credited as its terms require.

Educational content

Definitions, translations, examples and the pedagogical ranking are Sentari content, by Pengram. They are not copied from a third-party dictionary.

Language data

Lexical senses and relations. WordNet® 3.0, © 2006 Princeton University. WordNet supplies stable sense identifiers and lexical relations; Sentari selects the useful senses and writes its educational definitions, translations and examples separately. WordNet 3.0 License.

Pronunciation. American pronunciations derive from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary; Sentari converts ARPABET to IPA. CMUdict License.

CEFR levels. The CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.6. Compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies. Retrieved from https://www.cefr-j.org/download.html on 06/09/2026. Direct word-and-part-of-speech matches come from this list; the other levels are pedagogical estimates derived from word frequency, not certified CEFR levels. CEFR-J source.

Sentari frequency methodology

The final educational rank is computed by Sentari from transformed and normalized signals. The application receives the final rank only. It does not distribute source texts, source counts, per-source contributions or raw frequency tables.
